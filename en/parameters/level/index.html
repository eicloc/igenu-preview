Measurement parameters

Level and interface

Related instruments

Industrial measurement01
IG9000

Ultrasonic Level Transmitter

Ultrasonic time-of-flight measurement monitors levels in liquids and bulk materials without contact with the medium. Integrated and separate arrangements, signal analysis and temperature compensation support installation in water treatment and storage tanks, with configuration options matched to the measurement point.

Industrial measurement02
IG9500

Submersible Level Transmitter

A submersible pressure-based transmitter measures liquid level using a silicon sensing element in a sealed stainless-steel housing. Selectable output options support monitoring of tanks, water systems and pumping installations, with wetted materials matched to the measured liquid.

Industrial measurement03
IG9600

Ultrasonic Sludge Interface Monitor

Ultrasonic echo timing identifies the sludge interface in settling and thickening tanks. A separate probe, integrated temperature compensation and local display support monitoring in wastewater treatment and waterworks sedimentation processes, with configuration adapted to the installation.
