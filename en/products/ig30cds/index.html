IG30CDS Conductivity Sensor

Measure conductivity in water treatment and industrial process water with a choice of cell constants. Digital communication and stored calibration settings support system integration and routine operation, while the supplied mounting arrangement and flow cell simplify installation.

Overview

Product features:
◆ Fast response time, reaching 95% in 15 seconds
◆ Digital sensor, RS-485 output, standard Modbus RTU protocol, not affected by transmission distance, better stability
◆ The sensor is made of Ryton material, which has a longer service life and reduces maintenance and downtime
◆ Quick response, accurate measurement, reliable data, and convenient operation
◆ Internal electrodes can measure extremely weak signals without polarization effects
◆ Internal integrated memory, can still remember the calibration and setting information stored even after power failure
Application areas:
Surface water, municipal sewage, environmental monitoring, industrial process control, pollution sources, industrial wastewater, tap water, biopharmaceuticals, beer and beverage, petroleum and petrochemical, metallurgy
