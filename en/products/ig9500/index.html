IG9500 Submersible Level Transmitter

A submersible pressure-based transmitter measures liquid level using a silicon sensing element in a sealed stainless-steel housing. Selectable output options support monitoring of tanks, water systems and pumping installations, with wetted materials matched to the measured liquid.

Overview

Product features:
◆ Input based measurement method
◆ All stainless steel structure design, small size, light weight
◆ Choose stable and reliable diffusion silicon pressure sensors with excellent repeatability and stability
◆ Multiple output modes to choose from


Technical Parameter

Measurement range: 0-3/5/10/... 100m H20
Power supply: 24VDC
Output: 0/4-20mA, 0-10V, RS485 (optional)
Accuracy: ＜ 0.1%F.S.
Protection level: IP68
Long term stability: ± 0.2% F.S./year
Medium temperature:- 20℃ ~70℃
Sensor material: 316L
Temperature compensation range: 0-50 ℃
Full range temperature error: ± 0.3% F.S
Overload pressure: 2.5 times the full-scale pressure
