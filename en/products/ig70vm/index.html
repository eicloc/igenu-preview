IG70VM Thermal Gas Mass Flowmeter

Thermal sensing measures gas mass flow using a heated reference element and a temperature comparison. With no moving sensing components and selectable signal interfaces, the instrument supports gas-flow monitoring in petrochemical, power generation and metallurgical processes.

Overview

Product features:
◆A true quality flowmeter that does not require temperature and pressure compensation for gas flow measurement, making measurement convenient and accurate. Can obtain the mass flow rate or standard volume flow rate of gas
◆ Wide range ratio, capable of measuring gases with flow rates ranging from 100Nm/s to 0.2Nm/s, and can be used for gas leak detection
◆Good seismic performance and long service life. The sensor has no moving parts or pressure sensing components, and is not affected by vibration on measurement accuracy
◆ Easy installation and maintenance. If on-site conditions permit, non-stop installation and maintenance can be achieved
◆Using RS-485 communication or HART communication, factory automation and integration can be achieved

Application areas:

Petrochemical, Environmental Protection, Water Treatment, Thermal Power, Smelting, Medical
