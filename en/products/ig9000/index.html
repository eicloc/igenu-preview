IG9000 Ultrasonic Level Transmitter

Ultrasonic time-of-flight measurement monitors levels in liquids and bulk materials without contact with the medium. Integrated and separate arrangements, signal analysis and temperature compensation support installation in water treatment and storage tanks, with configuration options matched to the measurement point.

Overview

Product features:
◆ Integrated or split type options, suitable for different on-site installation requirements
◆The host can be selected as single channel or dual channel, and can achieve functions such as multi-point measurement and alarm
◆ Simple to use, reliable results, interface operation instructions can reduce operational errors
◆The probe material is available in POM, PVDF, PTFE options, suitable for various working conditions
◆ Internally integrated temperature sensor, capable of real-time temperature compensation for sound velocity

Application areas:

Government wastewater, industrial wastewater, environmental monitoring, monitoring of drinking and surface water, industrial processes
