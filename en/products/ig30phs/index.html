IG30PHS Digital pH Electrode

Digital pH measurement combines a gel-filled electrode, double-junction reference and integrated temperature sensing. Modbus communication supports connection to compatible controllers, while calibration options and the supplied mounting bracket and flow cell support installation and routine operation.

Overview

Product features:
◆ Rich configuration to meet various working conditions requirements
◆ Built in temperature sensor, automatic temperature compensation
◆ Automatic identification of standard solution, optional 1-point, 2-point standard solution calibration, and 1-point sample calibration
◆ Digital sensor, standard Modbus RTU protocol, not affected by transmission distance, better stability
◆The sensor is made of Ryton material, which has a longer service life and reduces maintenance and downtime
◆ Large capacity microporous double salt bridge makes the probe more resistant to toxicity and has a longer service life
◆ Thick and high impedance bubble with strong structural strength, not easily damaged by particle impact
◆ Shielded shell packaging, not affected by environmental electromagnetic interference
Application areas:
Surface water, municipal sewage, environmental monitoring, industrial process control, pollution sources, industrial wastewater, tap water, biopharmaceuticals, beer and beverage, petroleum and petrochemical, metallurgy
Technical parameters:
Measurement range: 0-14pH; Temperature:- 5-105℃
Resolution: 0.01 pH, 0.1 ℃
Accuracy: ± 0.01 pH
Repeatability: 0.01 pH
Working environment: 0-95 ℃
Response time: up to 95% in 30 seconds
