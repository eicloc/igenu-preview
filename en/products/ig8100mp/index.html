IG8100MP Portable Gas Detector and Alarm

A portable gas detector with pumped sampling and sensor options selected for the target gases. A color display presents measurement information, while audible, visual and vibration alarms support on-site awareness during mobile gas monitoring and inspection work.

Overview

Product features:

◆2.5-inch high-definition color screen, three display modes, capable of displaying curve graphs
◆ Equipped with a standard um level filter, it can filter out 99% of oil, water, dust and impurities, making it easier to operate and use
◆ Supports 1-4 types of sensors, millisecond level response, high accuracy
◆Pump suction sampling, suitable for any scenario
◆Triple alarm (sound and light, vibration), not easy to ignore
