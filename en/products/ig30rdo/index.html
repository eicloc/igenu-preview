IG30RDO Optical Dissolved Oxygen Sensor

Fluorescence-based sensing measures dissolved oxygen without electrolyte replacement or sensor polarization. Integrated temperature compensation and digital communication support continuous water monitoring, with air and sample calibration options for use in treatment, environmental and aquaculture applications.

Overview

Product features:
◆ Built in temperature sensor, automatic temperature compensation
◆Fluorescence method, no need to replace membrane and electrolyte, air calibration, simple and fast
◆ Digital sensor, RS-485 output, standard Modbus RTU protocol, not affected by transmission distance, better stability ◆ The sensor is made of titanium alloy material, with a longer service life, reducing maintenance and downtime
◆ No polarization required, fast response time
◆ Not affected by pH fluctuations CO2、 Interference from sulfides, hydrogen sulfide, sulfates, chlorides, ammonia nitrogen, and heavy metals
◆ Not affected by flow velocity interference and able to withstand severe impacts
Application areas:
Municipal sewage, industrial wastewater, environmental monitoring, monitoring of drinking and surface water, industrial processes, aquaculture, biological processes
Technical parameters:
Range: 0~20mg/L or 0~200% saturation, 0~65 ℃
Measurement principle: Fluorescence method
Measurement accuracy: ± 0.1 mg/L when below 5 mg/L
When it exceeds 5 mg/L, it is ± 0.2 mg/L
Temperature: ±0.1°C
Response time: 95% achieved in 37 seconds at 20 ℃
Repeatability: ± 0.1mg/L
Resolution: 0.01mg/L
Maximum pressure: 23bar @ 50 ℃
Maximum flow rate: No flow rate requirement
Calibration methods: saturated air calibration, 1-point sample calibration, zero point calibration, 1-point temperature calibration
Electrode body material: titanium alloy
Threaded interface: standard 3/4NPT
Sensor communication: MODBUS RS-485 
Temperature electrode: PT1000
Electrode form: digital electrode
Communication interface: ModbusRS485
Storage temperature:- 15~60℃ 
Working temperature: 0~65 ℃ (non freezing)
Protection level: IP68
Cable length: Standard 10m, extendable
Sensor size: 151mm * 26mm
