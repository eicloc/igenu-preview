IG3500 Residual Chlorine Analyzer

DPD colorimetry measures residual chlorine in water for supply and process monitoring. The analyzer combines optical interference compensation, automatic cleaning and data storage, with relay outputs for control integration and an optional remote communication module for connected operation.

Overview

Product features:
◆ DPD standard colorimetric method
◆ Long path optical structure, not easily contaminated, absorbance detection resolution can be greater than 0.001Abs
◆ Dual speed technology, automatically compensates for turbidity, chromaticity, and bubble interference
◆ Unique fluid structure with low maintenance requirements
◆ Relay output, can lock the chlorine pump to achieve automatic chlorine addition
◆ Optional 4G remote transmission module, wireless data upload
◆ Comes with storage function for easy viewing of records
Application areas:
Municipal tap water, pipeline monitoring, food and beverage, drinking and surface water monitoring, industrial processes
Technical parameters:
Range: 0~10mg/L
Measurement principle: DPD colorimetric method
Measurement accuracy: ± 1% of the measured value or 0.01mg/L, whichever is greater
Detection limit: 0.01mg/L
Measurement mode: continuous measurement, periodic measurement (adjustable measurement interval)
Display: 70mm × 48mm LCD display
Calibration: default calibration curve
Reagent remaining alarm: Yes
Data Storage: Historical Records
Automatic cleaning function: available
Reagent replacement cycle: 1 month (measured in 5 minutes)
Communication: RS485MODBUS 
Output: 4-20mA
Relay: 2 channels, alarm can be set
Power supply: 220-230VAC, 50/60Hz
Sample flow rate: 250-500mL/min
Sample inlet pressure: 0.1~5bar
Sample temperature: 5~45 ℃
Working environment: 5-95%, non condensing
Instrument size: 350mm * 186mm * 460mm (length * width * height)
Weight: 9kg
Installation method: Wall mounted
