IG9400 Ultrasonic Open-Channel Flowmeter

Non-contact ultrasonic level sensing calculates flow through configured open-channel weirs and flumes. A separate probe and display arrangement provides instantaneous and accumulated flow information for applications such as wastewater discharge points, irrigation and municipal water engineering.

Overview

Product features:
◆ Split type, suitable for different on-site installation requirements
◆ Automatic adjustment of transmission power based on flow rate, advanced digital model calculation, anti-interference design
◆ Easy to use, reliable results, interface operation instructions can reduce operational errors
◆ The probe material is available in POM, PVDF, and PTFE, suitable for various working conditions
◆ Internally integrated temperature sensor, capable of real-time temperature compensation for sound velocity

Technical parameters:

Instantaneous flow rate: determined by the type and specifications of the measuring weir tank used
