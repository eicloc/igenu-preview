IG3000 Display Multichannel Controller

Bring compatible digital and analog water sensors together in a multichannel controller with a clear display and menu navigation. Configurable outputs, relays and communication options support process integration, while an output-hold function assists with sensor maintenance.

Overview

Product features:
◆ Strong compatibility, able to connect digital and analog sensors from Igno company
◆90 * 70 large screen LED display, user-friendly interface
◆Large size UV resistant and anti-corrosion PS shell, suitable for various harsh working conditions.
◆4-20mA output with control function, capable of PID control of external devices
◆ Optional multiple relays, Modbus RTU, DeviceNet, Modbus RS232, CANopen, EthermetIP, Modbus TCP, TCP/IP communication, etc., can meet various on-site requirements
◆ Multi channel controller, capable of connecting one or more sensors
◆ Output hold function, convenient for sensor maintenance
◆IP66 protection level, CE certification.
