IG5500 Pressure Transmitter

A silicon sensing element and signal-conditioning circuit provide pressure measurement for compatible gases and liquids. A compact housing, selectable outputs and process connections support installation in water treatment, hydraulic equipment and industrial process control applications.

Overview

Product features:
◆Widely used media, can measure oil, water, paste, etc., with certain anti-corrosion ability
◆Good linearity, high accuracy, high stability, long lifespan
◆ Small size, light weight, easy to install

◆ Wide range, corrosion-resistant, suitable for various media

Technical parameters:
Measurement medium: gas, liquid, oil, etc
Measurement form and range: gauge pressure (G) - maximum (0-60) MPa, Minimum (0-0.5) Kpa
Absolute pressure (A) - maximum (0-40) MPa, Minimum (0-5) KPa
Negative pressure (B) - (-0.1~40) MPa, Minimum (-0.2~0.2) KPa
Environmental temperature:- 10~65℃ 
Environmental humidity: 5-100% relative humidity
Accuracy level: 0.1%
Overload capacity: 2-5 times the range
Long term stability: less than 0.2% FS/60 months
Power supply: 24VDC
Output signal: (4-20) mA (two/three wire system) (optional HART protocol output) （0～10/20）mA
（0～5）V、（1～5）V、（0～10）V、 (Optional) RS485
Display mode: digital display
Measurement rate: 0.2S
Working temperature:- 40～80℃
Compensation temperature:- 20～70 ℃
Process connection: M20 × 1.5 external thread (Ø 3 internal hole), or user specified
Protection level: IP67
Electromagnetic compatibility: RF protection complies with EN61326
