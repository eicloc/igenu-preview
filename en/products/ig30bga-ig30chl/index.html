IG30BGA/IG30CHL Algae and Chlorophyll Sensor

Fluorescence sensing enables direct monitoring of blue-green algae and chlorophyll without sample extraction or chemical reagents. Digital communication supports continuous water monitoring, with a cleaning-brush option to help manage deposits on the optical sensing surface.

Overview

Product features:
◆ Adopting titanium alloy corrosion-resistant shell; NPT3/4 threaded interface for easy installation;
◆ With self-cleaning function, it can remove attachments from water samples without maintenance;
◆ The sample does not require processing or extraction, is non-destructive, reagent free, and pollution-free; Direct measurement is simpler than traditional analysis methods
◆ Accurate measurement, low detection limit, and good long-term stability;
◆ Ultra bright LED light source, improving signal-to-noise ratio and enhancing stability;
◆ Equipped with calibration record storage function, plug and play, no calibration required for the first use.
◆ Digital sensor, RS-485 output, supports MODBUS
◆ Can be continuously monitored online, real-time control of water quality dynamics
Application areas:
Monitoring of drinking water sources, warning of algal blooms, intake of water plants, environmental monitoring
Technical parameters:
Measurement range: blue-green algae: 0-200000 cells/mL
                                  Chlorophyll: 0-1000ppb
Measurement principle: UV fluorescence
Measurement accuracy: ± 3% of reading or 0.1ppb
Repeatability: ± 3%
Calibration method: single point calibration, based on experimental results
Medium requirements: flow rate: 0-3m/s; Pressure: 0-100psi
Sensor material: 316L or POM
Sensor communication: MODBUS RS-485 
Storage temperature:- 15~50℃ 
Working temperature: 0~50 ℃ (non freezing)
Self cleaning: automatic scraping brush (optional)
Cable length: Standard 10m, extendable
Sensor size: 176mm * 22mm
