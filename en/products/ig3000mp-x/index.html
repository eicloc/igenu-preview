IG3000MP-X Multiparameter Water Analyzer

Build a water monitoring system around interchangeable digital probes for parameters such as pH, dissolved oxygen, conductivity and turbidity. Probe connections and stored calibration data support field replacement, while an automatic cleaning arrangement helps limit surface deposits.

Overview

Product features:
◆ Digital sensor, RS485 output, supports MODBUS
◆All calibration parameters are stored in the sensor, and each probe comes with a waterproof connector for easy insertion and replacement
◆Equipped with an automatic cleaning device, it can effectively remove surface stains from sensors, prevent microbial growth, and provide more accurate and lower maintenance
◆ Optional dissolved oxygen, conductivity, turbidity pH、ORP、 Chlorophyll, blue-green algae, water oil and other sensors, suitable for long-term online monitoring
◆ Multi in one structural design, capable of simultaneously connecting 7 probes and measuring 8 parameters

Application areas:

Environmental monitoring, monitoring of drinking and surface water, industrial wastewater, sewage, domestic wastewater, sewage, groundwater, lake water
