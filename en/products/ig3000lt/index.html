IG3000LT Low-Range Turbidity Analyzer

Designed for low-level turbidity measurement in drinking water and treatment systems, this analyzer combines optical detection with a flow cell that removes bubbles. Its modular arrangement supports calibration, cleaning and servicing, with temperature displayed alongside turbidity.

Overview

Product features:
◆ Meets and exceeds the requirements of USEPA 180.1 method
◆ Stable and efficient bubble removal system
◆ Compact structure, small footprint
◆Low operating costs
◆135mL flow cell - low hysteresis, high sensitivity
◆Simultaneously measure and display temperature
◆ Easy cleaning and maintenance
◆ Output locking function, easy to maintain
Application areas:
Drinking water treatment system, monitoring of drinking water pipe network, membrane treatment process, food and pharmaceutical engineering, monitoring of canned water system, wastewater discharge outlet
Technical parameters:
Range: 0-100NTU
Resolution: 0.0001NTU
Accuracy: ± 1% of the reading, or 0.008 NTU, whichever is greater (less than 20 NTU);
± 2% of the reading, or 0.015NTU, whichever is greater (greater than 20NTU)
Response time: 30 seconds to reach 90%
Measurement unit: NTU
Temperature unit: ℃
Calibration: zero point calibration, 1-point standard sample calibration, temperature calibration, slope calibration
Display: Large screen LED display, 7-key menu navigation
Signal filtering: 0-100S
Analog output: 2-channel 4-20mA
Digital Communication: MODBUS/RS485,MODBUS/RS232，Profibus，DeviceNet、CANopen、EthermetIP、ModbusTCP,TCP/IP
Relay: 2-channel FromC, 5A@250VAC
Sample flow rate: 200-750mL/min
Water temperature:- 5~50℃
Environmental temperature:- 20~60℃
Light source: White light (tungsten lamp)
Light chamber: anodized aluminum
Circulation pool: ABS plastic, 135ml
Sensor material: quartz, anodized aluminum
Inlet pipe interface: 1/4
Inlet pipe interface: 3/8
Power requirements: 100-230VAC, 50/60Hz
Controller size: 200mm * 200mm * 70mm
Sensor and flow cell size: 210mm * 140mm * 100mm
Power consumption: ≤ 10W
