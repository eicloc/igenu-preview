IG5200 Temperature Transmitter

Combine a resistance temperature detector or thermocouple with signal conversion and local indication in an integrated transmitter. An electrical output proportional to temperature supports connection to monitoring equipment, while the local display assists with inspection at the measurement point.

Overview

Product features:
◆ Accurate signal and long-distance transmission (up to 1000 meters)
◆ High precision, anti-interference, good long-term stability, maintenance free
◆ The transmitter can display measurements on site
◆ Range: -200 ℃ to 1200 ℃; Conventional accuracy: ± 0.25%
Technical parameters:
Measurement range:- 200-1200℃
Temperature measurement range of thermistor:<Pt100>-200~450 ℃< Cu50>-50～150℃
Thermocouple temperature measurement range：< K>  0℃-1200℃；          < E>  0℃-800℃；
                                                                                <S>  0℃-1600℃              <B>  0℃-1800℃
Measurement accuracy:<thermal resistance>± 0.25%; ± 0.5%； < Thermocouple>± 0.75%
Output signal:4-20mA
Warm float:  ± 0.025%/℃
Time drift: less than ± 0.5% per year
Environmental temperature: -10~85 ℃
Power supply voltage: 24V DC ± 10%
Load capacity: 0-500 Ω
Display range:  -1999～1999
Protection level:  IP65
Explosion proof level:  dII BT6
Material of protective tube: 1Cr18Ni9Ti or ceramic or steel jade tube
