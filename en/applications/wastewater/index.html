Wastewater

Understand water and sludge throughout treatment.

Wastewater applications bring together different measurement tasks. Instruments for pH, dissolved oxygen, suspended solids, sludge concentration and nutrients support monitoring at relevant process stages. Confirm the measurement range and installation conditions for each instrument.

Display Multichannel Controller

IG30RDO

Optical Dissolved Oxygen Sensor

Fluorescence-based sensing measures dissolved oxygen without electrolyte replacement or sensor polarization. Integrated temperature compensation and digital communication support continuous water monitoring, with air and sample calibration options for use in treatment, environmental and aquaculture applications.

The digital probe combines optical compensation with a sapphire window, while an optional cleaning brush helps manage deposits during continuous monitoring at the measurement point.
