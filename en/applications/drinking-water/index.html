Applications

Drinking water

Measurement across treatment and distribution.

Drinking water monitoring spans the inlet, treatment process, plant outlet and distribution network. Explore instruments for pH, turbidity, conductivity and residual chlorine according to each model’s specifications.

The quality of drinking water is measured at different stages: at the entry to the treatment plant, during the treatment process, at its exit from the treatment plant and all along the distribution network.

Related instruments

Water analysis01
IG30PHS

Digital pH Electrode

Digital pH measurement combines a gel-filled electrode, double-junction reference and integrated temperature sensing. Modbus communication supports connection to compatible controllers, while calibration options and the supplied mounting bracket and flow cell support installation and routine operation.

Water analysis02
IG30RDO

Optical Dissolved Oxygen Sensor

Fluorescence-based sensing measures dissolved oxygen without electrolyte replacement or sensor polarization. Integrated temperature compensation and digital communication support continuous water monitoring, with air and sample calibration options for use in treatment, environmental and aquaculture applications.

Water analysis03
IG30SS/SOL

Suspended Solids and Sludge Concentration Sensor

Infrared scattered-light sensing monitors suspended solids and sludge concentration in water and wastewater processes. The digital probe combines optical compensation with a sapphire window, while an optional cleaning brush helps manage deposits during continuous monitoring at the measurement point.

Water analysis04
IG30UVNO3

UV Nitrate Nitrogen Sensor

Ultraviolet absorption measures nitrate nitrogen directly in water without chemical reagents or a separate sampling system. Selectable optical paths, interference compensation and automatic cleaning support online deployment in drinking water, environmental monitoring and wastewater treatment applications.

Water analysis05
IG3000LT

Low-Range Turbidity Analyzer

Designed for low-level turbidity measurement in drinking water and treatment systems, this analyzer combines optical detection with a flow cell that removes bubbles. Its modular arrangement supports calibration, cleaning and servicing, with temperature displayed alongside turbidity.

IG3000MP-X

Multiparameter Water Analyzer

Build a water monitoring system around interchangeable digital probes for parameters such as pH, dissolved oxygen, conductivity and turbidity. Probe connections and stored calibration data support field replacement, while an automatic cleaning arrangement helps limit surface deposits.
