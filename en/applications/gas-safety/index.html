OSHA Has Determined Asphyxiation to Be the Leading Cause of Death in Confined Spaces. Air is oxygen deficient whenever concentration is less than 19.5% O2.

Poisoning

As an LEL sensor become poisoned the sensors surface and the sensors pores will be coated with oxidized silicone.

A silicone poisoned sensor will first loose its ability to detect methane, while not exhibiting any noticeable inhibition to detect other combustible gases .

As poisoning progresses the the LEL sensor will eventually lose is ability to detect any combustible gases.

Related instruments

Gas detection01
IG8100

Fixed Gas Detector and Alarm

Configured for the required target gas, this fixed detector provides continuous concentration monitoring and local audible and visual alarms. Analog and digital outputs connect detection points with compatible control and monitoring systems in the selected installation.

Industrial measurement02
IG70VM

Thermal Gas Mass Flowmeter

Thermal sensing measures gas mass flow using a heated reference element and a temperature comparison. With no moving sensing components and selectable signal interfaces, the instrument supports gas-flow monitoring in petrochemical, power generation and metallurgical processes.

Gas detection03
IG8100MP

Portable Gas Detector and Alarm

A portable gas detector with pumped sampling and sensor options selected for the target gases. A color display presents measurement information, while audible, visual and vibration alarms support on-site awareness during mobile gas monitoring and inspection work.

Gas detection04
IG8300VOC

Online Odor and Gas Monitoring System

Monitor configured odor-related gases through a multisensor system with sample pretreatment, temperature compensation and automatic calibration functions. An integrated display, data storage and communication options support connected environmental monitoring at wastewater treatment, chemical production and waste-handling sites.
