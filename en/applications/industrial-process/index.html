Related instruments

Water analysis01
IG3000

Display Multichannel Controller

Bring compatible digital and analog water sensors together in a multichannel controller with a clear display and menu navigation. Configurable outputs, relays and communication options support process integration, while an output-hold function assists with sensor maintenance.

Water analysis02
IG30PHS

Digital pH Electrode

Digital pH measurement combines a gel-filled electrode, double-junction reference and integrated temperature sensing. Modbus communication supports connection to compatible controllers, while calibration options and the supplied mounting bracket and flow cell support installation and routine operation.

Water analysis03
IG30RDO

Optical Dissolved Oxygen Sensor

Fluorescence-based sensing measures dissolved oxygen without electrolyte replacement or sensor polarization. Integrated temperature compensation and digital communication support continuous water monitoring, with air and sample calibration options for use in treatment, environmental and aquaculture applications.

Water analysis04
IG30SS/SOL

Suspended Solids and Sludge Concentration Sensor

Infrared scattered-light sensing monitors suspended solids and sludge concentration in water and wastewater processes. The digital probe combines optical compensation with a sapphire window, while an optional cleaning brush helps manage deposits during continuous monitoring at the measurement point.

Gas detection05
IG8100

Fixed Gas Detector and Alarm

Configured for the required target gas, this fixed detector provides continuous concentration monitoring and local audible and visual alarms. Analog and digital outputs connect detection points with compatible control and monitoring systems in the selected installation.

Industrial measurement06
IG1000

Electromagnetic Flowmeter

Electromagnetic flow sensing measures conductive liquids, including water and process slurries. Inline installation and electrical outputs support integration into process monitoring, with material and connection options selected to suit the application and the measured medium.

Water analysis07
IG3000LT

Low-Range Turbidity Analyzer

Designed for low-level turbidity measurement in drinking water and treatment systems, this analyzer combines optical detection with a flow cell that removes bubbles. Its modular arrangement supports calibration, cleaning and servicing, with temperature displayed alongside turbidity.

Water analysis08
IG30CDS

Conductivity Sensor

Measure conductivity in water treatment and industrial process water with a choice of cell constants. Digital communication and stored calibration settings support system integration and routine operation, while the supplied mounting arrangement and flow cell simplify installation.

Water analysis09
IG30CLS

Residual Chlorine Sensor

Using a constant-potential electrochemical method, this sensor measures residual chlorine and chlorine dioxide in water. A dedicated flow cell maintains sample flow, while the design avoids colorimetric reagents and routine replacement of membranes or electrolyte.

Water analysis10
IG30HT

High-Range Turbidity Sensor

Infrared scattered-light sensing measures turbidity in process water, surface water and treatment applications. The digital probe combines optical compensation with a sapphire window and offers an optional cleaning brush to reduce deposits during ongoing monitoring.

Water analysis11
IG30OL

Oil-in-Water Sensor

Ultraviolet fluorescence detects oil in water for applications including petrochemical wastewater and oilfield reinjection monitoring. Optical filtering and a self-cleaning arrangement help manage measurement interference and deposits, while digital communication connects the probe to monitoring systems.

Water analysis12
IG30PH01

Analog pH Electrode

An analog pH electrode for water and process monitoring, combining a gel-filled design with a double-junction reference arrangement. An integrated temperature sensor supports compensation, and the mounting bracket and flow cell provide options for installation at the measurement point.

Water analysis13
IG30PTCO

Water Color Sensor

Optical absorption measures water color against the platinum-cobalt color scale. Automatic turbidity compensation and a cleaning brush support online monitoring, while stored calibration settings and digital communication simplify connection to water monitoring systems at the installation site.

Water analysis14
IG30RPS

Digital ORP Electrode

A digital ORP electrode for oxidation-reduction monitoring in water and industrial processes. Its platinum sensing element and double-junction reference are complemented by digital communication, sample or standard calibration, and a mounting bracket and flow cell for installation.

Water analysis15
IG30RPS01

Analog ORP Electrode

An analog ORP electrode for water treatment and process monitoring, using a platinum sensing element and double-junction reference. Sample and standard calibration options, together with a mounting bracket and flow cell, support installation and routine measurement.

Water analysis16
IG3254COD

UV COD and TOC Sensor

Ultraviolet absorption supports online COD and TOC measurement without reagent dosing. Turbidity compensation, calibration options and an available automatic cleaning system help adapt the digital probe to water monitoring, with results interpreted according to the selected calibration.

Water analysis17
IG3500

Residual Chlorine Analyzer

DPD colorimetry measures residual chlorine in water for supply and process monitoring. The analyzer combines optical interference compensation, automatic cleaning and data storage, with relay outputs for control integration and an optional remote communication module for connected operation.

Industrial measurement18
IG4200

Online Total Solids Analyzer

Microwave transmission measures total solids concentration directly in water samples without prior separation or settling. Continuous readings and control-system connectivity support process monitoring in wastewater treatment, chemical production, pharmaceutical manufacturing and food processing applications.

Industrial measurement19
IG5500

Pressure Transmitter

A silicon sensing element and signal-conditioning circuit provide pressure measurement for compatible gases and liquids. A compact housing, selectable outputs and process connections support installation in water treatment, hydraulic equipment and industrial process control applications.

Industrial measurement20
IG70VM

Thermal Gas Mass Flowmeter

Thermal sensing measures gas mass flow using a heated reference element and a temperature comparison. With no moving sensing components and selectable signal interfaces, the instrument supports gas-flow monitoring in petrochemical, power generation and metallurgical processes.

Gas detection21
IG8100MP

Portable Gas Detector and Alarm

A portable gas detector with pumped sampling and sensor options selected for the target gases. A color display presents measurement information, while audible, visual and vibration alarms support on-site awareness during mobile gas monitoring and inspection work.

Gas detection22

Online Odor and Gas Monitoring System

Monitor configured odor-related gases through a multisensor system with sample pretreatment, temperature compensation and automatic calibration functions. An integrated display, data storage and communication options support connected environmental monitoring at wastewater treatment, chemical production and waste-handling sites.

Industrial measurement23
IG9000

Ultrasonic Level Transmitter

Ultrasonic time-of-flight measurement monitors levels in liquids and bulk materials without contact with the medium. Integrated and separate arrangements, signal analysis and temperature compensation support installation in water treatment and storage tanks, with configuration options matched to the measurement point.

Industrial measurement24
IG9500

Submersible Level Transmitter

A submersible pressure-based transmitter measures liquid level using a silicon sensing element in a sealed stainless-steel housing. Selectable output options support monitoring of tanks, water systems and pumping installations, with wetted materials matched to the measured liquid.
